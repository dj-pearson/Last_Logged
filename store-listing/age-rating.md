# Age rating

Last Logged has no user-generated public content, no ads, no in-app chat,
no violence, and no mature themes. Expected ratings: **4+ (iOS) / Everyone (Android)**.

## Play Store — Content rating questionnaire (IARC)

- Violence → No
- Sex → No
- Language → No
- Controlled substances → No
- Gambling → No
- Miscellaneous → No
- Users interact with each other → **No** (the app has no social features)
- Shares user-provided content → **No** (all data private to the user)
- Shares user's location → **No**
- Digital purchases → **Yes** (subscriptions + lifetime)

Expected rating: **Everyone** (matches IARC rating for "Productivity app with IAP, no social, no sensitive content").

## US state privacy / ads declarations

- Ads: **None served inside the app.**
- Third-party analytics: **TelemetryDeck (privacy-preserving, no device identifiers).**
- Kids: App is not directed at children under 13. Marketed to adults who want
  to track home / car / pet / personal maintenance. COPPA does not apply.

## Release notes for reviewers

> Last Logged is a recurring task tracker. There is no user-generated public
> content, no chat, no social features, and no advertising. Subscriptions are
> the only monetization, handled through Apple / Google billing via RevenueCat.
